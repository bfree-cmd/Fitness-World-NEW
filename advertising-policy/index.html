COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

Advertising & Sponsorship Policy

Revenue can support the publication, but it does not buy a favorable Fitness World verdict.

Editorial independence

Advertisers and sponsors do not control our evidence labels, article conclusions or practical verdicts.

Clear separation

Paid placements and sponsored material should be clearly identified so readers can distinguish commercial content from independent editorial content.

Affiliate relationships

Some links may eventually earn Fitness World a commission. Affiliate eligibility does not determine whether a product, category or claim receives a positive assessment.

No guaranteed coverage

Payment does not guarantee editorial coverage, a favorable review or inclusion in What Actually Works.

Reader trust first

If a commercial arrangement creates a conflict that would make an independent evaluation unreasonable, we should not present that evaluation as independent editorial work.
