HOW IT WORKS

What the checker can and can’t tell you.

It searches claims Fitness World has already reviewed and published. It does not write a new answer from your wording.

See our evidence standards →

If your claim matches a review

  • The Fitness World verdict and evidence rating
  • What supports the claim and what is still uncertain
  • A practical takeaway and a link to the full investigation

What it won’t do

  • Rate a claim we have not reviewed. You can request an investigation instead.
  • Generate a new AI or personalized verdict
  • Diagnose a condition or give medical advice
