# Creatine hair-loss claim review

Draft for review only. No production publication authorized.

| Claim | Decision | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creatine causes hair loss | Soften: available evidence has not established causation | 2025 trial; 2021 narrative review |
| 2009 DHT study proves hair loss | Reject inference; retain hormone finding with limitations | 2009 rugby crossover study; hair not measured |
| Direct hair trial found no significant difference | Keep: 38 men completed 12 weeks at 5 g/day | 2025 randomized trial |
| 2021 review settles hair outcomes | Qualify as narrative hormone-evidence review, predating direct trial | 2021 JISSN paper |
| Results apply to everyone indefinitely | Reject; add duration, population and dose limits; disclose industry ties | 2025 methods and disclosures |
| New hair loss warrants assessment | Keep: dermatologist can investigate causes | AAD official guidance |
