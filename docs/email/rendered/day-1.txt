Most diets fail because they hand you a meal plan you can't keep.

The Reset uses a method instead. Every meal gets four parts:

1. Protein (eggs, yogurt, beans, fish, poultry)

2. Produce (any fruit or vegetable you'll actually eat)

3. A fiber-rich carb (oats, whole grains, brown rice)

4. A healthy fat (olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado)

Try it for breakfast tomorrow: yogurt, berries, oats and walnuts. Four parts, one bowl.

It works with your culture, your budget and your preferences. Nothing to buy.
