THE EVIDENCE REVIEW PROCESS

How an Evidence Project Works

Important health questions deserve more than a quick answer.

Choose the Question

Community interest helps identify meaningful health questions.

Search the Evidence

Relevant research, reviews and clinical evidence are identified.

Assess Quality

We examine study design, consistency, limitations and potential bias.

Compare the Findings

We identify where evidence agrees, conflicts or remains uncertain.

Publish the Results

Findings are translated into practical Fitness World resources.

Keep It Updated

Important projects can be revisited as stronger evidence emerges.

INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

Research first. Conclusions second.

Evidence Projects are structured to keep commercial influence separate from the review and publication process.

No Industry Funding

Evidence Projects are not funded by companies whose products could be materially affected by the investigation.

Community Supported

Reader support helps fund independent research and editorial work.

Transparent Methods

We explain the evidence reviewed and make uncertainty clear when the research does not provide a simple answer.

Findings Aren't for Sale

Supporting a project never purchases or determines a particular conclusion.
