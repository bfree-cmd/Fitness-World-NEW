MARKETING CHECK

Be skeptical of products borrowing the GLP-1 label.

Over-the-counter products marketed as “GLP-1 supplements” should not be treated as substitutes for prescription GLP-1 medicines.

INSUFFICIENT GLP-1 Supplements: Which Marketing Claims Should You Question?→
2026 GLP-1 UPDATE

GLP-1 treatment is no longer only an injection conversation.

Current FDA labeling includes an oral tablet formulation of Wegovy (semaglutide). For Fitness World, the important consumer-health point is that a different delivery format does not remove the nutrition and muscle-preservation questions that can accompany substantial weight loss.

What stays relevant

Protein adequacy, resistance training, fiber, food tolerance and overall nutritional quality still deserve attention. Medication choice, dosing and side-effect management remain clinical decisions.
