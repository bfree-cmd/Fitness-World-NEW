WHAT “RESET” MEANS HERE

Built to be repeatable, not extreme.

Reset refers to your routines and habits — not to your body or physiology. There are no pounds to lose, no toxins to flush and no guaranteed results. Just repeatable actions, scored for feedback, not as a grade.

Progress, not perfection

A score is feedback, not a grade. You are not expected to score 7/7 every day.

Safety first

Natural does not automatically mean risk-free. Form, dose, medicines, health status and preparation all matter.
