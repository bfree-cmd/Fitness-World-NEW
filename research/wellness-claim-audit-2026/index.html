What the first 30 Evidence Index claims show

This report analyzes the first 30 claims selected for the Fitness World Evidence Index. It is not a population survey and does not estimate what people believe. It describes the evidence labels in our own launch dataset.

Snapshot

30claims analyzed
11stronger evidence
7limited or mixed
4unsupported / insufficient

The launch set is intentionally mixed: it includes established training and nutrition questions, popular supplement decisions, wearable claims and highly marketed wellness ideas. The result is a decision library rather than a list of products Fitness World endorses.

Method

We selected 30 published Fitness World investigations that already contain an Evidence Check. Each entry was normalized into one of the public Evidence Standards categories while retaining the article's original conclusion. No rating was generated from keyword popularity, affiliate value or commercial opportunity.

Territory distribution: Stay Strong 9; Choose Smarter 13; Live Healthier 8.

Practical verdict distribution: Worth considering 6; Depends 17; Skip / Question 7.

What this can and cannot tell us

The dataset can show how Fitness World is classifying the specific claims it has investigated. It cannot tell us how common those beliefs are, how consumers behave, or whether the same distribution would appear in a random sample of wellness claims. Those questions require separate research.
