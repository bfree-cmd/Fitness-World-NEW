Progressive training remains the foundation

TRAININGDo You Need Heavy Weights to Build Muscle After 40?No single rep range owns muscle growth. Heavier loads are useful for strength, while moderate and lighter loads can also build muscle when sets are challenging enough.→MUSCLEHow Long Does It Take to Build Muscle After 40?Strength can improve before visibly larger muscles appear. The useful timeline is measured in months, not in a promised number of pounds per week.→
