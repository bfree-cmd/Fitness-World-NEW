Supplement Evidence Index

Popular supplements often carry dozens of claims. Fitness World separates what is supported, what is uncertain and what matters before you spend.

HOW TO USE THIS INDEX

Judge the claim, not the supplement.

A supplement can have strong evidence for one outcome and weak evidence for another. Fitness World separates the claim, the evidence and the practical decision instead of assigning one universal score.

1Claim

What outcome is being promised?

2Evidence

How consistently is that outcome supported?

3Context

Who was studied, at what dose and for what purpose?

4Decision

Does using or buying it solve a real problem?
