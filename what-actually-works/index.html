THE FITNESS WORLD SYSTEM

We rate claims, not hype.

A supplement or habit can have stronger evidence for one outcome and limited evidence for another. Fitness World avoids giving one vague universal score when the underlying claims differ.

STRONGER More consistent supporting evidenceMODERATE Useful evidence with important limitsMIXED Findings are inconsistentLIMITED Evidence is early, sparse or uncertain
